Officer's assessment (draft)

The site lies wholly outside the defined settlement boundary. Policy S5 is therefore engaged, and the proposal must be judged against the specific list of forms of development capable of approval in that location, together with the design and landscape tests it sets out.

Members should note the site does not fall within any of the categories at S5(1)(a)–(f), and no case for isolated dwellings under national policy has been made in the supporting statement.
